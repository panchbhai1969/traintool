You are evaluating a crop of one tooth from a dental photograph for extrinsic stain. Extrinsic stain can be generalised or localised. Use adjacent enamel and the gingival margin as context. Distinguish stain from shadow, calculus, restoration margins, and caries. Be conservative about the visual findings. 
Interdental dark yellow discoloration should be considered as stains as well

If the dark area is sharply demarcated, opaque, metallic/glossy, or has a contour/texture suggestive of restorative material, do not label it as extrinsic stain.

VERY VERY IMPORTANT :

Metallic Amalgam and restoration should not be considered as stains.
Visible dentin resulting from attrition should not be classified as extrinsic staining.
Calculus and decayed tooth should not be classified as extrinsic stains
The color of the stains should be prominent enough . light yellowish-white specks  discoloration can be skipped
General discoloration on the grooves should not be considered as stains
uniform off-white to pale yellow coloration gradient can be considered as stains
